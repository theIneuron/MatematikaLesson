# Grade 4 English glossary (`en-GB`)

This glossary is the source of truth for English wording inside Grade 4 lessons. Use the same term in visible text, feedback, accessibility labels and narration.

## Style

- Use British spelling: `metre`, `centimetre`, `litre`, `practise` (verb), `practice` (noun).
- Use short, direct sentences suitable for a nine- or ten-year-old.
- Keep Uzbek names unchanged: Madina, Zaynab, Alisher, Bekzod and others.
- Say numbers, fractions, operations and units in words in audio. Symbols and digits may remain in visible mathematical notation.
- Use `Correct.` and `Not quite.` for neutral feedback. Avoid praise such as `Great job!`.
- Use `Choose`, `Find`, `Enter`, `Compare`, `Check` and `Continue` for instructions.

## Audio forms

- `+`, `−`, `×`, `÷`, `=` → `plus`, `minus`, `times`, `divided by`, `equals`.
- `1/2`, `1/3`, `1/4` → `one half`, `one third`, `one quarter`.
- Other fractions use cardinal numerator + ordinal denominator: `3/5` → `three fifths`, `7/10` → `seven tenths`.
- Decimals use `point`: `2.35` → `two point three five` unless the lesson explicitly teaches place-value reading.
- Area units include `square`: `12 cm²` → `twelve square centimetres`.
